# Jurist-AI
### Intelligent Legal Analysis & Outcome Likelihood Estimation System

Jurist-AI is a legal technology project focused on making legal research and case analysis more accessible through Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning.

The system is designed to analyse legal case descriptions, identify relevant legal issues, retrieve similar judgments from Indian courts, and estimate possible case outcomes based on patterns in historical decisions.

The aim is to reduce the time spent on manual legal research and provide useful, evidence-based insights from existing case law.

## What We're Building

- **Case Analysis:** Process legal case descriptions and identify important information.
- **Legal Document Processing:** Extract relevant terms, entities, and legal references from documents.
- **Precedent Retrieval:** Find similar cases and judgments from available Indian legal sources.
- **Outcome Estimation:** Use historical case data and machine learning to estimate possible outcomes.
- **Result Presentation:** Present relevant judgments, extracted information, and analysis in an organized interface.
